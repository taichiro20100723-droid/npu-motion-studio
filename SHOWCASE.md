# Share your impossible middle

Made something strange with NPU Motion Studio? Post the result so other people can remix the idea.

## The fastest way

1. Export a short MP4 or GIF.
2. Include the A image, B image, prompt, hardware, and generation time.
3. Open a [Showcase issue](https://github.com/taichiro20100723-droid/npu-motion-studio/issues/new?template=showcase.yml) and attach the result.
4. Share the issue link on X, Bluesky, Reddit, or your project page.

## Prompts that make good demos

- “A paper sketch folds into a real product, line by line, while the camera slowly pushes in.”
- “A rusted robot unfolds into a bright orange dog; metal plates soften into fur.”
- “A ruined concrete room grows plants, windows, and warm lights until it becomes a greenhouse.”
- “A handwritten Japanese title dissolves into neon symbols and reforms as a clean logo.”
- “A dark mechanical bird melts into black ink, then flies out of the final frame.”

## What to include

Please avoid private photos and assets you cannot redistribute. The most useful showcase posts include:

- A and B images or a contact sheet
- the exact motion prompt
- quality mode and video length
- CPU / NPU / GPU model
- generation time and driver notes
- what worked, what drifted, and what you would try next

The goal is not a perfect benchmark. Weird, beautiful failures are welcome too.
